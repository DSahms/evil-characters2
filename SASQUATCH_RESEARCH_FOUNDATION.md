## Geographic home range

**Core (keep him here):** southeastern Mount St. Helens — upper Muddy River, Pine Creek, Ape Canyon, Muddy fan, toward Swift Reservoir / Lewis River. Human towns he would smell or hear at a distance: Cougar, then farther Carson / Home Valley / Columbia Gorge. Eastward quiet country toward Mt. Adams is plausible seasonal travel. North face / Spirit Lake is **not** his home; that is a different drainage.

**Do not send him to:** Ohio, Florida, New Jersey, California (Patterson-Gimlin), British Columbia as personal experience. Those can be overheard human talk only.

He does not start life knowing “Washington,” “Skamania County,” or “Gifford Pinchot.” His map is ridges, berry slopes, the muddy water, the mountain that rumbles, machine-smell, and other families’ quiet.

## Birth-region circa 1950 (L1)

- Forest around St. Helens is already inside a national forest: Columbia NF (1908), renamed **Gifford Pinchot June 15, 1949** (Truman proclamation / USFS). That rename happens the year before he is born. He would not care about the name.
- Logging, miners, Forest Service roads, hunters, and CCC-era infrastructure already exist. This is not empty wilderness; it is managed woods with loud seasons.
- Upper Muddy / SE flank is old forest, lava, canyons, huckleberry, elk country — not a town. Birth in wilderness is geographically honest.

## Local Sasquatch lore (L2)

**Ape Canyon, summer 1924 (before his birth).** Prospectors (Fred Beck and others) told *The Oregonian* of ~7-ft “apemen,” a cabin stoned at night, a wounded one falling into the gorge. Forest Service rangers Huffman and Welch found nothing; they argued the tracks could be faked. The gorge kept the name. Adjacent **Ape Cave** is a lava tube named in the same cultural aftershock, not a proven den.

This is the strongest *local* story. If his people were in that drainage, 1924 is family weather, not his memory.

**Skamania County Ordinance 69-01 (April 1, 1969) (L1 as a human law; L2 as motive).** Commissioners cited reported sightings and armed “investigators.” Willful slaying of a Sasquatch-like creature was made a felony ($10k / 5 years). **1984** amendment: downgraded to a misdemeanor, county called a “Sasquatch Refuge.” Chamber of Commerce later said the point was also stopping people from shooting bearded elk hunters in GPNF. He would not read the ordinance. He might notice more armed strangers after humans got excited.

**Name “Sasquatch”:** Anglicized from Halkomelem (Sts’ailes / related Coast Salish) *sasq’ets* — **not** a Cowlitz word he would be born with. Southern Washington tribes have their own being-stories; do not flatten them into one mascot. Do not have him call himself “Sasquatch” unless the interview earns it from overhearing humans.

**Distant noise he should not “remember as his own”:** Patterson-Gimlin film, Bluff Creek, CA, 1967. Humans in his woods would talk after that. He was ~17. That is rumor on the wind, not a California trip.

## Family / social model (L2 theory, light L3 hypothesis)

Researchers and witness databases disagree: solitary adults vs small family groups; nocturnal vs crepuscular; omnivore forager vs occasional hunter/scavenger; wood knocks and whoops as communication vs coincidental.

**Working hypothesis for this one person (not proven biology):** born to a mother; early years in a small family or extended cluster; increasing independence as a juvenile; mostly solitary adult with seasonal overlap of others; strong human-avoidance plus curiosity; diet of berries, shoots, fish, carrion, raided human garbage when desperate. Wood knocks / whoops exist in the report literature — do not make him a radio station.

**Do not lock yet:** father’s presence, siblings, mates, whether anyone died in 1980, burial of dead, literacy, speech to humans. StoryKeeper should find those.

## Human encroachment across his lifetime (L1)

- **1950s:** GPNF name is new; timber harvest and forest roads continue; Spirit Lake already a recreation magnet (north of him).
- **1964:** Wilderness Act; Mt. Adams / Goat Rocks wilderness (east/north of core) — machines stop at lines he did not draw.
- **1960s–70s:** more logging roads, clear-cuts, hunters, chainsaws, helicopters; after 1967, more “ape hunters” with rifles (the ordinance’s actual human problem).
- **1980 eruption** (below).
- **Aug 26, 1982:** Mount St. Helens National Volcanic Monument (~110,000 acres). Scientists, buses, viewpoints. Salvage logging and “the greatest good” colliding with a moonscape.
- **1984:** more wilderness designations in GPNF (Indian Heaven, Trapper Creek, etc.).
- **1997:** Johnston Ridge Observatory (north — tourist flood he can avoid).
- **2004–2008:** mountain reawakens (dome growth, steam) — L1 unrest; L3 how it felt is for the interview.
- **2000s–2020s:** GPS, phones, trail cameras, drones, social media “proof” culture, recreation pressure on FR 83 / Ape Canyon trail (bikes), large fire years in the West. Encroachment is smell, light, and cameras, not a county line.

He would never think “I moved into a monument because it was protected.” He would think: west got louder, trees vanished, roads multiplied; the quieter ground happened to be the ground humans later fenced with signs.

## Major environmental events (L1)

- Cascades winters, floods, droughts — use only if a specific dated event is needed later.
- **May 18, 1980, 8:32 a.m. PDT:** magnitude 5.1, north-flank collapse, largest historic landslide, lateral blast **north**, 240 sq mi forest flattened, summit dropped ~1,300 ft, Spirit Lake buried/transformed, Toutle/Cowlitz/Columbia clogged with sediment.
- **SE / Muddy / Pine / Ape Canyon (HIS country):** not the north blast killing zone. Hot pyroclastic surge on the east flank stripped Shoestring Glacier; lahars down Ape Canyon, Muddy fan, Muddy River, Pine Creek. Pine Creek lahar reached Swift Reservoir ~9:00 a.m.; Muddy ~9:40 a.m. Trees sheared/debarked; Muddy River bridge destroyed; lower Muddy choked with logs; Swift rose ~2.6 ft from debris. USGS circ. 850-B; WA DNR roadside geology; USFS Lahar Viewpoint.

**Plausible 1980 experience (not predetermined death):** If he was on a ridge **out of the canyon bottom**, he could live and still have the world end: ground restless for weeks (March–May earthquakes, north bulge), animals wrong, then heat, dark, ash, a river that was no longer a river. Family in the gorge that morning might be gone. That is a **possibility** for StoryKeeper, not a fact in this file.

He would not say “8:32 a.m. May 18.” He might later learn humans’ date.

## Plausible lifetime movement corridor

Stay in a **C** shape around the mountain’s south and east:
upper Muddy / Ape Canyon country → down toward Lewis / Swift when food or fire forces it → Wind River / toward Columbia only as a dangerous edge → east toward quieter Adams country in hard years → **never** as a resident of the north blast zone after 1980 (that country was dead, then a science park).

Post-1980: monument tourists on the north and east viewpoints; his old SE woods recovering slowly (mudflow succession). He can return to familiar drainages that look wrong for decades.
